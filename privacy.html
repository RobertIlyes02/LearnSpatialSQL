Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 August 2026.

The short version

Your SQL runs inside your own browser. GeoSQL has no application server that executes queries, so the code you write is never transmitted anywhere — unless you press Submit while signed in, which saves that one submission so it can appear on the leaderboard.

If you don't create an account

Nothing about you is stored on our side. Which problems you've solved are kept in your browser's localStorage, on your device only. Clearing your browser data erases it, and we never see it.

If you create an account

Accounts are handled by Supabase. We store:

  • Your email address — used only to sign you in. It is never displayed publicly and never shared or sold.
  • A display name for the leaderboard. This is a generated pseudonym such as SwiftPolygon420, never your email. If you sign in with GitHub, your public GitHub username is used instead. You can change it at any time on the Leaderboard page.
  • Your submissions: the problem, the SQL you submitted, whether it passed, and how long it took. Only the timing and your display name are public — the SQL itself is readable only by you and is blocked at the database level from anyone else.

Passwords are hashed by Supabase and are never visible to us.

Sign-in with GitHub

If you use GitHub sign-in, GitHub shares your email address and public username with us. GeoSQL requests the minimum scope (user:email) and cannot read your repositories or act on your behalf.

Analytics

We use privacy-preserving, cookieless analytics to count page views and see which sites link to us. No cookies are set, no cross-site tracking happens, and no individual is profiled. This is why you don't see a cookie banner — there are no tracking cookies to consent to.

What we never do

  • Sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • Publish your email address anywhere.
  • Send marketing email. The only mail you may receive is sign-in or password confirmation.

Deleting your data

Email support@geosql.dev and ask. Your account, profile, and submissions will be deleted permanently. No justification needed and no attempt to talk you out of it.

Third parties

GeoSQL relies on: Supabase (accounts and database), Vercel and Cloudflare (hosting and delivery), and jsDelivr (serving the DuckDB WebAssembly runtime). Each sees standard technical data such as your IP address in order to serve requests.

Contact

Questions or corrections: support@geosql.dev.
