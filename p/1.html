Point in Polygon

Easy Predicates ST_WithinPolygon

Solve this in your browser →

You have a table of city parks (polygons) and a table of incident reports (points). Find all incidents that fall inside any park.

Goal

Return the incident_id and park_name for every incident that lies within a park boundary, ordered by incident_id.

Example Output

incident_id  park_name
-----------  --------------------
1            Riverside Park
3            Central Commons
5            Riverside Park

Tables

ColumnTypeDescription
parks.park_idINTEGERPrimary key
parks.park_nameVARCHARName of park
parks.geomGEOMETRYPolygon boundary (EPSG:4326)
incidents.incident_idINTEGERPrimary key
incidents.geomGEOMETRYPoint location

DuckDB Spatial Functions

Load the extension first: LOAD spatial;

Use ST_Within(point, polygon) to test containment, or ST_Intersects.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 Join the two tables with a spatial predicate instead of an equality condition.
💡 Hint 2 ST_Within(a.geom, b.geom) returns TRUE when geometry A is completely inside B.
💡 Hint 3 Don't forget LOAD spatial; at the top of your query.
