Spatial Join Two Layers

Medium Joins ST_IntersectsJOIN

Solve this in your browser →

You have a table of delivery zones (polygons) and a table of warehouses (points). Some warehouses are positioned right on a zone boundary and may intersect more than one zone.

Goal

For every warehouse, return warehouse_name, zone_name for each zone it intersects. If a warehouse intersects no zone, omit it. Order by warehouse_name, then zone_name.

Example Output

warehouse_name   zone_name
---------------  ---------
Depot Alpha      North
Depot Alpha      East
Depot Beta       South

Tables

ColumnTypeDescription
zones.zone_idINTEGERPrimary key
zones.zone_nameVARCHARZone label
zones.geomGEOMETRYPolygon
warehouses.warehouse_idINTEGERPrimary key
warehouses.warehouse_nameVARCHARWarehouse label
warehouses.geomGEOMETRYPoint

Unlike ST_Within, ST_Intersects also matches geometries that merely touch or overlap a boundary — which is why one warehouse can match multiple zones here.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 This is a standard inner join — but with a spatial predicate as the ON condition instead of an equality.
💡 Hint 2 A point sitting exactly on a shared edge will intersect both adjacent polygons — that's expected, not a bug.
