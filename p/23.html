Busiest US Flight Routes

Medium Measurement read_parquetST_DistanceGROUP BY

Solve this in your browser →

A Parquet file holds 1,500 US domestic flights with origin/destination airport codes and coordinates.

Goal

Find the 5 busiest route pairs by flight count. Treat JFK→LAX and LAX→JFK as the same route by normalising direction with LEAST / GREATEST.

Only include routes with at least 5 flights. Break ties by average distance descending.

Expected Output

airport_1  airport_2  total_flights  avg_distance_km
---------  ---------  -------------  ---------------
EWR        PHL        19             128
ORD        SFO        16             2964
BOS        MSP        16             1804
CLT        LAS        15             3077
ATL        SEA        14             3505

Parquet file: ./data/us_flights_sample.parquet

ColumnTypeDescription

callsignVARCHARAirline + flight number

destinationVARCHARDestination IATA code
origin_lat / origin_lonDOUBLEOrigin airport coordinates
dest_lat / dest_lonDOUBLEDestination airport coordinates
distance_kmDOUBLEHaversine great-circle distance
altitude_ftINTEGERCruise altitude
speed_ktINTEGERAirspeed in knots

Hints

💡 Hint 1 LEAST(origin, destination) and GREATEST(origin, destination) give you a canonical alphabetical order regardless of flight direction.
💡 Hint 2 Use HAVING COUNT(*) >= 5 to filter out thin routes before sorting.
💡 Hint 3 ROUND(AVG(distance_km), 0) gives a clean integer-like distance. Order by total_flights DESC, avg_distance_km DESC.
