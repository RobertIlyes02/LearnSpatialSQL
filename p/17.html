LineString Intersection

Medium Set Ops ST_IntersectionST_AsText

Solve this in your browser →

A port authority tracks ferry routes and a north-south shipping lane, all stored as LineStrings. For safety planning they need the exact point where each ferry route crosses the shipping lane.

Goal

Return route_name and crossing_point (the intersection as WKT text via ST_AsText) for every ferry route that intersects the shipping lane, ordered by route_name.

Expected Output

route_name      crossing_point
--------------  --------------
Coastal Loop    POINT (5 20)
Harbor Express  POINT (5 5)
Island Hopper   POINT (5 5)

Tables

ColumnTypeDescription
ferry_routes.route_nameVARCHARRoute label
ferry_routes.geomGEOMETRYRoute LineString
shipping_lane.geomGEOMETRYThe shipping lane LineString

ST_Intersection(a, b) returns the shared geometry of two inputs — for two crossing lines, that's a POINT. ST_AsText converts a geometry back to WKT for display.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 Join ferry routes to the shipping lane with ST_Intersects as the join condition — routes that don't cross are filtered out automatically.
💡 Hint 2 ST_AsText(ST_Intersection(f.geom, s.geom)) gives the crossing point as readable WKT.
