Drone Tracks from Pings

Medium Geometry ST_MakeLineST_LengthGROUP BY

Solve this in your browser →

A drone fleet logs GPS pings with a sequence number. Reconstruct each drone's flight track as a LineString and measure how far it flew.

Goal

For each drone, connect its pings in sequence order into a track, and return drone_id and track_length (rounded to 2 decimal places), ordered by track_length descending.

Expected Output

drone_id  track_length
--------  ------------
3         16.0
2         13.0
1         10.0

Table: drone_pings

ColumnTypeDescription
drone_idINTEGERWhich drone
seqINTEGERPing order within the flight
geomGEOMETRYPing location (point)

ST_MakeLine(list) builds a LineString from a LIST of points. DuckDB's list(x ORDER BY y) aggregate controls element order — critical here, since a shuffled track would zigzag and measure wrong.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 Aggregate points per drone with list(geom ORDER BY seq) — the ORDER BY inside the aggregate is what keeps the track in flight order.
💡 Hint 2 Full chain: ROUND(ST_Length(ST_MakeLine(list(geom ORDER BY seq))), 2).
