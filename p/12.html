Simplify Dense Coastline

Medium Geometry ST_SimplifyST_NPoints

Solve this in your browser →

A GIS analyst has a table of coastline segments with very high vertex density — too detailed for small-scale map rendering. They want to simplify each segment and compare the vertex count before and after.

Goal

Return segment_name, points_before (vertex count of the original), and points_after (vertex count after simplifying with a tolerance of 1.8), ordered by segment_name.

Example Output

segment_name   points_before  points_after
-------------  -------------  ------------
North Coast    6              2
South Cove     5              3

Table: coastlines

ColumnTypeDescription
segment_nameVARCHARName of coastline segment
geomGEOMETRYLineString geometry (many vertices)

ST_NPoints(geom) counts the number of vertices. ST_Simplify(geom, tolerance) uses the Douglas-Peucker algorithm to reduce vertex count while preserving overall shape.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 Apply ST_NPoints twice: once on the original geom and once on ST_Simplify(geom, 1.8).
💡 Hint 2 You can use both in the same SELECT — no subquery needed.
