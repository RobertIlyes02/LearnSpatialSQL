Simplify Dense Geometry

Medium Geometry ST_SimplifyST_Area

Solve this in your browser →

Cartographers simplify jagged lake shorelines before rendering small-scale maps — but simplification distorts the shape. Quantify that distortion by comparing each lake's area before and after simplification.

Goal

Return lake_name, area_before, and area_after (area after ST_Simplify with tolerance 1.0), both rounded to 2 decimal places, ordered by lake_name.

Expected Output

lake_name   area_before  area_after
----------  -----------  ----------
Lake Arrow  56.3         57.5
Lake Bell   98.0         100.0

Table: lake_shores

ColumnTypeDescription
lake_nameVARCHARLake name
geomGEOMETRYShoreline polygon (jagged, many vertices)

ST_Simplify(geom, tolerance) removes vertices whose deviation is under the tolerance (Douglas-Peucker). Simplifying a polygon can grow or shrink its area — that's the distortion you're measuring.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 Compute ST_Area twice in one SELECT: once on geom, once on ST_Simplify(geom, 1.0).
💡 Hint 2 Wrap both with ROUND(..., 2) to match the expected precision.
