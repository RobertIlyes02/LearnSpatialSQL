Merge Coverage Areas

Medium Aggregation ST_UnionST_GeomFromText

Solve this in your browser →

A telecoms company has a table of cell tower coverage polygons. Some towers overlap. The company wants to know the total number of distinct merged coverage blobs after unioning all polygons together.

Goal

Union all coverage polygons into a single geometry, then count how many distinct non-overlapping sub-geometries it contains. Return a single row with column coverage_blobs.

Example Output

coverage_blobs
--------------
2

Table: towers

ColumnTypeDescription
tower_idINTEGERPrimary key
geomGEOMETRYCoverage polygon

ST_Union can be used as an aggregate just like SUM — no GROUP BY needed to merge all rows into one geometry. The result may be a MULTIPOLYGON if the inputs don't all touch. ST_NumGeometries(geom) counts how many sub-geometries a multi-geometry contains.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 ST_Union(geom) as an aggregate merges all polygons, dissolving shared boundaries.
💡 Hint 2 ST_NumGeometries(ST_Union(geom)) gives you the count of disconnected blobs in one expression.
