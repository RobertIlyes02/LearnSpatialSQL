Snap Points to Road

Medium Snapping ST_ShortestLineST_StartPointST_X

Solve this in your browser →

GPS devices often record positions slightly off the road network due to signal error. Given a table of GPS pings and a single road segment, snap each ping to its closest point on the road.

Goal

Return ping_id, snapped_x and snapped_y (coordinates of the closest point on the road, rounded to 2 decimal places), ordered by ping_id.

Example Output

ping_id  snapped_x  snapped_y
-------  ---------  ---------
1        3.00       0.00
2        7.00       0.00
3        10.00      0.00

Tables

ColumnTypeDescription
road.geomGEOMETRYThe road as a LineString
gps_pings.ping_idINTEGERPrimary key
gps_pings.geomGEOMETRYRaw GPS point

ST_ShortestLine(a, b) returns the shortest LineString connecting two geometries. Its start point lies on a, so ST_StartPoint(ST_ShortestLine(line, point)) is the point on the line closest to your input. Wrap with ST_X() / ST_Y() to extract coordinates.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 Cross join GPS pings with the road (JOIN road ON TRUE) so you can reference both geometries in the same row.
💡 Hint 2 ST_ShortestLine(r.geom, p.geom) gives the connecting segment; its start point is the snapped location on the road.
💡 Hint 3 Full chain: ROUND(ST_X(ST_StartPoint(ST_ShortestLine(r.geom, p.geom))), 2). (PostGIS users: this is what ST_ClosestPoint does — it isn’t in the browser DuckDB build yet.)
