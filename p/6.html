Convex Hull of Cluster

Medium Aggregation ST_ConvexHullST_Collect

Solve this in your browser →

A table of sensor pings records GPS points from a fleet of drones, each tagged with a mission_id. For each mission, compute the convex hull polygon enclosing all of its pings, and report its area.

Goal

Return mission_id and hull_area (planar area of the convex hull), ordered by mission_id.

Example Output

mission_id  hull_area
----------  ---------
1           50.0
2           12.5

Table: pings

ColumnTypeDescription
ping_idINTEGERPrimary key
mission_idINTEGERGroups pings by mission
geomGEOMETRYPoint location

You'll need to aggregate points per mission into a single multi-point geometry before computing a hull. ST_Collect takes a LIST of geometries (e.g. via list(geom) after a GROUP BY) and combines them into one.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 First GROUP BY mission_id and use list(geom) to collect all points per mission into an array.
💡 Hint 2 Wrap that list with ST_Collect(...) to get one multi-geometry, then ST_ConvexHull(...), then ST_Area(...).
💡 Hint 3 Full chain: ST_Area(ST_ConvexHull(ST_Collect(list(geom)))).
