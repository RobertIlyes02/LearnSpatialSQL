Nearest Restaurant per Hotel

Hard Distance read_parquetST_DistanceLATERAL

Solve this in your browser →

A Parquet file contains 43 NYC points of interest across hotels, museums, parks, and restaurants.

Goal

For each hotel, find its nearest restaurant and the approximate walking distance in metres.

Use a LATERAL join to run a correlated subquery per hotel row. Approximate metres with ST_Distance(...) * 111000.

Expected Output

hotel                 nearest_restaurant  dist_m
--------------------  ------------------  ------
The Bowery Hotel      Wildair             522
The High Line Hotel   Don Angie           1100
The Plaza             The Smith           717

Parquet file: ./data/nyc_pois.parquet

ColumnTypeDescription
poi_idINTEGERRow ID
nameVARCHARVenue name
categoryVARCHAR'hotel' | 'museum' | 'park' | 'restaurant'
latDOUBLELatitude (WGS84)
lonDOUBLELongitude (WGS84)

Hints

💡 Hint 1 A LATERAL join lets the subquery reference columns from the outer row. Syntax: FROM outer_table t, LATERAL (SELECT ... FROM ... ORDER BY ... LIMIT 1) sub.
💡 Hint 2 Inside the LATERAL, filter for category = 'restaurant', order by ST_Distance(ST_Point(t.lon, t.lat), ST_Point(r.lon, r.lat)) ASC, LIMIT 1.
💡 Hint 3 Multiply the degree distance by 111000 to get approximate metres: ROUND(ST_Distance(...) * 111000).
