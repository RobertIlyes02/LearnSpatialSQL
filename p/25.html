Sensors Near the Pipeline

Easy Predicates ST_DWithinST_Distance

Solve this in your browser →

A gas company monitors a pipeline (LineString) with field sensors (points). Sensors more than 50 units from the pipeline are out of radio range and need relocating.

Goal

Return the sensor_id and sensor_name of every sensor within 50 units of the pipeline, ordered by sensor_id.

Use ST_DWithin — it's clearer (and often faster) than computing ST_Distance and comparing.

Expected Output

sensor_id  sensor_name
---------  -----------
1          S-Alpha
2          S-Bravo
4          S-Delta

Tables

ColumnTypeDescription
pipeline.geomGEOMETRYThe pipeline LineString
sensors.sensor_idINTEGERPrimary key
sensors.sensor_nameVARCHARSensor label
sensors.geomGEOMETRYSensor point

ST_DWithin(a, b, dist) returns TRUE when the geometries are within dist of each other. Note that distance to a LineString is measured to the nearest point on the line — a sensor below the line counts just like one above it.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 Cross join sensors with the pipeline (only one pipeline row), then filter with ST_DWithin(s.geom, p.geom, 50).
💡 Hint 2 Careful: S-Delta sits at y = -45, below the pipeline — but it's still within 50 units of it.
