Flood Risk Buffer

Medium Buffering ST_BufferST_Within

Solve this in your browser →

A city has mapped a flood zone polygon. Properties within 500 metres of the flood zone boundary are considered at-risk and must be notified.

Goal

Return the property_id and address of every property that falls within a 500-unit buffer of the flood zone, ordered by property_id.

Example Output

property_id  address
-----------  ---------------
1            12 River St
2            45 Valley Rd
3            7 Creek Ave
4            88 Lowland Dr

Tables

ColumnTypeDescription
flood_zone.geomGEOMETRYThe flood zone polygon
properties.property_idINTEGERPrimary key
properties.addressVARCHARStreet address
properties.geomGEOMETRYPoint location

ST_Buffer(geom, distance) expands a geometry outward by distance units in all directions, returning a new polygon. You can then use ST_Within or ST_Intersects to test containment against that expanded shape.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 First expand the flood zone using ST_Buffer(geom, 500), then test which properties fall within it.
💡 Hint 2 You can use a subquery or CTE: SELECT ST_Buffer(geom, 500) FROM flood_zone to get the buffered shape, then join properties against it.
