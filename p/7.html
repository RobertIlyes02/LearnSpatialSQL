Polygon Difference Delta

Hard Set Ops ST_DifferenceCTE

Solve this in your browser →

A city re-zoned some land. The zones_old and zones_new tables hold the boundary polygon for the same zone before and after the change. Find the area of land that was removed from each zone (present in the old boundary but not the new one).

Goal

Return zone_name and removed_area (area of old - new), ordered by removed_area descending. Only include zones where removed_area > 0.

Example Output

zone_name  removed_area
---------  ------------
Zone B     20.0
Zone A     10.0

Tables

ColumnTypeDescription
zones_old.zone_nameVARCHARZone identifier (shared key)
zones_old.geomGEOMETRYOld boundary polygon
zones_new.zone_nameVARCHARZone identifier (shared key)
zones_new.geomGEOMETRYNew boundary polygon

ST_Difference(a, b) returns the part of geometry a that does not overlap with b.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 Join old and new boundaries on zone_name, then compute ST_Difference(old.geom, new.geom).
💡 Hint 2 Wrap the difference in ST_Area(...) to get a number, then filter with HAVING or a WHERE on a CTE since you can't reference a SELECT alias directly in some clause positions.
💡 Hint 3 A CTE makes this readable: compute the area first, then filter and sort in the outer query.
