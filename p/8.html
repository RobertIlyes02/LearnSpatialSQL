Reverse Geocode Grid

Medium Geocoding ST_XST_YFLOOR

Solve this in your browser →

Instead of true reverse geocoding, this exercise snaps each sighting point to a coarse 1 x 1 degree grid cell, identified by its lower-left corner coordinates. This is a common first step before aggregating sparse geospatial data (e.g. for heatmaps).

Goal

Return sighting_id, grid_x, grid_y where grid_x/grid_y are the floored integer coordinates of the 1x1 cell containing the point. Order by sighting_id.

Example Output

sighting_id  grid_x  grid_y
-----------  ------  ------
1            5       12
2            5       12
3            -3      8

Table: sightings

ColumnTypeDescription
sighting_idINTEGERPrimary key
geomGEOMETRYPoint (lng, lat)

Use ST_X(geom) and ST_Y(geom) to pull out raw coordinates, then FLOOR() to snap to the grid.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 ST_X(geom) gives longitude, ST_Y(geom) gives latitude.
💡 Hint 2 FLOOR(ST_X(geom))::INTEGER snaps to the cell's lower-left x-coordinate. Same idea for y.
