Perimeter of Land Parcels

Easy Measurement ST_BoundaryST_Length

Solve this in your browser →

A land registry stores parcel polygons. Fencing contractors need the perimeter of each parcel to estimate materials.

Goal

Return parcel_id and perimeter (total boundary length, rounded to 2 decimal places), ordered by perimeter descending.

Example Output

parcel_id  perimeter
---------  ---------
2          40.00
1          24.00

Table: parcels

ColumnTypeDescription
parcel_idINTEGERPrimary key
geomGEOMETRYParcel polygon

Two approaches work here: ST_Perimeter(geom) directly, or ST_Length(ST_Boundary(geom)) — ST_Boundary converts a polygon to its outer ring as a LineString, then ST_Length measures it.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 The simplest approach: ST_Perimeter(geom) returns the perimeter of a polygon directly.
💡 Hint 2 Alternatively, ST_Length(ST_Boundary(geom)) produces the same result and is good practice for understanding how boundary extraction works.
