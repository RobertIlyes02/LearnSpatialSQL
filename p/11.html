Neighbourhood Centroids

Easy Geometry ST_CentroidST_XST_Y

Solve this in your browser →

A city stores its neighbourhood boundaries as polygons. Urban planners need the centre point (centroid) of each neighbourhood to place service facilities.

Goal

Return name, cx (centroid longitude, rounded to 4 decimal places), and cy (centroid latitude, rounded to 4 decimal places) for every neighbourhood, ordered by name.

Example Output

name           cx       cy
-------------  -------  ------
Eastside       25.0     2.6667
Westside        5.0     3.3333

Table: neighbourhoods

ColumnTypeDescription
nameVARCHARNeighbourhood name
geomGEOMETRYBoundary polygon

ST_Centroid(geom) returns a POINT geometry at the geometric centre of the input. Use ST_X() and ST_Y() to extract the coordinates.

Hints

💡 Hint 1 Chain the functions: ST_X(ST_Centroid(geom)) gives you the x-coordinate of the centroid.
💡 Hint 2 Wrap with ROUND(..., 4) to match the expected output precision.
